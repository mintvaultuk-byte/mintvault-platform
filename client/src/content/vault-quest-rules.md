# Vault Quest Rules — v0.1 (Open Playtest)

> **Open playtest.** These rules are locked for playtesting, not for final print. They will change.

## 1. Game objective
Win by claiming 5 Seals. Seals are claimed by Knocking Out opposing Creatures. Knocking Out a Final / Stage 3 Creature claims 2 Seals instead of 1.

## 2. Deck size
Each player uses a 40-card deck.

## 3. Copy limit
A deck may contain up to 4 copies of a card with the same card name.

## 4. Element limit
A deck may contain cards from up to 2 elements. Neutral cards do not count towards this limit.

## 5. Opening hand
Each player draws 5 cards at the start of the game.

## 6. Starting board
Each player must start with 1 Baby / Stage 1 Creature as their Active Creature. Up to 2 additional Baby / Stage 1 Creatures may start in Reserve.

## 7. Reserve slots
Each player has 2 Reserve slots.

## 8. Core Bank
Each player has a Core Bank with a maximum of 10 Core.

## 9. Core unlock
During your Core Phase, unlock 1 additional Core until you reach the cap of 10.

## 10. Core ready
At the start of your turn, all unlocked Core readies and can be spent again that turn.

## 11. Attack permission
Attacking does not require Core permission. Attacks may have printed Core costs if the attack itself says so.

## 12. Turn sequence
Each turn follows: Ready Phase, Draw Phase, Core Phase, Action Phase, End Phase.

## 13. Ready Phase
Ready all your exhausted cards and ready all unlocked Core.

## 14. Draw Phase
Draw 1 card. The first player skips their first Draw Phase.

## 15. Core Phase
Unlock 1 Core, up to the Core cap of 10.

## 16. Action Phase
During your Action Phase you may play cards, Ascend Creatures, use effects, Shift, and attack.

## 17. End Phase
Resolve end-phase effects and clear temporary effects that last until End Phase.

## 18. Ascend rule
A Baby may Ascend into its Teen form. A Teen may Ascend into its Final form. You may not Ascend a Creature on the same turn it entered play or already Ascended.

## 19. Ascend cost
Teen / Stage 2 Ascend cost is 2 Core. Final / Stage 3 Ascend cost is 4 Core.

## 20. Ascend draw
When you Ascend, draw 1 card.

## 21. Ascend inheritance
Damage stays on the Creature when it Ascends. Status effects clear. Attached Relics stay attached unless the card says otherwise.

## 22. Shift
Once per turn, you may pay the Creature's Shift cost to swap your Active Creature with one of your Reserve Creatures.

## 23. Health
Damage counters stay on Creatures. If damage on a Creature is equal to or greater than its Health, it is Knocked Out.

## 24. Guard
Guard reduces incoming attack damage by the Guard value. Guard is visible and printed on the card.

## 25. Guard broken
If a Creature is Guard Broken, its Guard is ignored until End Phase.

## 26. Vulnerability
If an attack hits a Creature vulnerable to the attacker's element, the attack deals +2 damage.

## 27. Knock Out
When a Creature is Knocked Out, discard it and all cards under it. The attacking player claims Seal(s). The defending player draws 1 card.

## 28. Empty board loss
If you cannot place a Creature as your Active Creature when required, you lose the game.

## 29. Mulligan
If your opening hand has no Baby / Stage 1 Creature, reveal it, shuffle it into your deck, and draw a new 5-card hand. Your opponent draws 1 card for each mulligan you take.

## 30. Ties
If both players would win at the same time, the player with more Seals wins. If tied, the player whose turn it is loses.

## Quick start
1. Shuffle your 40-card deck.
2. Draw 5 cards.
3. Place 1 Baby Creature as your Active Creature.
4. You may place up to 2 Baby Creatures in Reserve.
5. Each player starts with 1 unlocked Core.
6. Play turns in this order: Ready, Draw, Core, Action, End.
7. Claim 5 Seals to win.
8. Knock Out a Baby or Teen to claim 1 Seal.
9. Knock Out a Final Creature to claim 2 Seals.
