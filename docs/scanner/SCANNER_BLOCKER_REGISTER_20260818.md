# Scanner final architecture blocker register

**Date:** 2026-08-18  
**Scope:** read-only final planning pass. This register creates no implementation approval and records no claim that the listed defects have been fixed.  
**Canonical plan:** [SCANNER_FINAL_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN_20260818.md](SCANNER_FINAL_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN_20260818.md)

## Severity and evidence rules

- **BLOCKER:** cannot ship the affected scanner path safely or operably.
- **HIGH:** material security, evidence-integrity, money, grading, or operational risk that must be fixed before release.
- **MEDIUM:** must be planned and bounded before scale; may be gated out of an initial limited release only with documented compensating control.
- **OWNER / EXTERNAL:** a decision or third-party prerequisite, not a software defect to guess around.

“Reproduction” is restricted to source/history/report evidence observed in this pass. No scanner, job, payment, email, staging, or production mutation was performed.

## Mandatory release closure order

1. Close `SFAP-001` through physical evidence, not a presumed `ippusbd` fix.
2. Close `SFAP-003`, `SFAP-004`, and `SFAP-005` together: lifecycle ownership and fixed-profile identity are one capture integrity boundary.
3. Close `SFAP-006` and `SFAP-008` with protected-grading approval before allowing the new detailed inspection path.
4. Close `SFAP-002` and `SFAP-007` before any partner distribution.
5. Close `SFAP-009` if—and only if—the watched contingency is implemented; otherwise retain its quarantine and document it as disabled.
6. Close `SFAP-010` and `SFAP-011` before a 5,000-station claim or rollout; complete `SFAP-012` before broad partner self-service recovery.
7. Obtain `SFAP-013` and `SFAP-014` decisions/proofs before live commercial launch.

No item may be closed merely by adding a test name. Closure evidence must identify the source commit/package, environment, input data/hardware state, exact assertions, results, logs/artifacts, reviewer, and rollback result where relevant.
