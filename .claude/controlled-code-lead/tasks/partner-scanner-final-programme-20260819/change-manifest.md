## Owner-locked credit purchase and zero-credit acceptance repairs — 2026-08-19

authoritative Checkout/intent across retry and restart, a hard zero-credit stop, and a live wallet
display. Independent read-only reviews identified four release defects in the existing UI/payment
path: a zero lock could be replaced with an old positive snapshot after a failed refresh, a checkout
was only tab/process idempotent, a restarted Scanner stopped reconciling a later grant, and a
previously created paid Checkout could be denied after its pack was disabled or its Price rotated.

This repair does **not** alter Stripe configuration, external Stripe objects, wallet rows, ledger
rows, reservations, Card Jobs, Oliver’s wallet, Pilot’s 499 available credits, MV280, or production.
The one database change is additive staging/payment-provenance metadata. Wallet credit still enters
only through the existing verified Stripe-webhook ledger boundary.
