# Mint Vault UK Ltd — Privacy Notice

**Version v1.0-2026-08-19 · Effective 19 August 2026**

This notice explains how Mint Vault UK Ltd (“MintVault”, “we”, “us”) uses personal information when you use mintvaultuk.com, contact us, submit cards for grading, or apply to take part in the MintVault Partner programme. It is written to explain the processing currently used by MintVault in clear language.

## 2. Information we collect

The information depends on how you interact with us. It can include:

- **Contact and correspondence information** — your name, email address, phone number, postal address and messages you send us.
- **Grading and submission information** — the details of cards submitted, declared values, service choices, photographs or scans, delivery details, certificate details and communications about a submission.
- **Payment and transaction information** — payment status, transaction references and billing information needed to administer a purchase. MintVault does not store full payment-card numbers; Stripe processes payment-card information.
- **Partner-application information** — business name, contact name and business email, town/city and postcode, business type, website or business social profile, reason for applying, and any optional qualification details you choose to provide. We also record the notice version and time acknowledged, a one-way duplicate-prevention key, safe campaign attribution and the application status.
- **Technical and security information** — information that our website, hosting and security systems process to operate and protect the service, such as IP address, browser/device information and request logs. The Partner-application record itself does not store IP address or user-agent information.
- **Information from service providers** — for example payment status from Stripe and delivery or email-delivery information needed to administer a service or contact you.

Please do not include special-category personal data, identity documents or payment-card details in a Partner application.

## 3. Why we use information and our lawful bases

We use personal information only where UK data-protection law allows us to do so.

| Activity | Purpose | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Providing grading, submission, certificate and customer-support services | To take steps you ask us to take and provide the service | Performance of a contract, or steps at your request before a contract |
| Taking payment and keeping financial records | To administer payment, refunds and accounting obligations | Contract and legal obligation, where applicable |
| Operating, securing and improving the site and service | To prevent abuse, investigate faults, protect systems and maintain records | Legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable service |
| Partner applications | To receive, assess and respond to an expression of interest; plan a measured Partner rollout; and prevent duplicate or abusive submissions | Legitimate interests in assessing prospective retail relationships and operating the application process fairly |
| Legal claims, fraud prevention and regulatory requests | To protect MintVault and others, and comply with applicable law | Legitimate interests and/or legal obligation, depending on the request |

The required fields in the Partner form are needed to receive and assess an application. You are not under a statutory obligation to provide them, but we cannot process the application without them. A Partner application is an expression of interest only: it does not create a Partner account, tenant, operational access, or automatic approval.

The required “I have read the Privacy Notice” checkbox is an acknowledgement that we provided this information at collection time. It is **not** consent for all application processing and it is not the lawful basis for reviewing the application.

## 4. Partner applications and marketing

MintVault uses Partner-application information to assess and respond to that application. We do **not** add Partner applicants to a marketing list or send Partner-programme marketing merely because they apply.

For card submissions, MintVault separately offers an unticked choice to feature a card in marketing such as social videos and weekly highlights. If you choose it, the card, certificate ID and grade may be shown publicly. We record that choice and you can withdraw it in your account settings or by contacting us; withdrawal excludes the card from future reel selection. That card-feature consent is separate from a Partner application and does not apply to Partner applicants.

If MintVault later offers other optional marketing communications, it will present a separate, unticked choice explaining the messages and how to withdraw. Where consent is required for electronic marketing, we will rely on that separate consent and record it. You can object to direct marketing at any time by contacting us; we will stop it.

## 5. Who may receive information

We use service providers that help us operate MintVault. Depending on the service, these include:

- Stripe for payment processing;
- Resend for transactional email delivery;
- Cloudflare for website infrastructure and file storage services;
- Neon for managed database hosting; and
- Fly.io for application hosting.

They process information only for the services they provide to us, except where a provider acts as a separate controller for its own service, such as Stripe’s handling of payment-card information. We may also share information with professional advisers, insurers, delivery providers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies or courts where necessary and lawful. We do not sell personal information.

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where a restricted transfer is required, MintVault uses a transfer mechanism permitted by UK data-protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Contact us if you would like information about the safeguards relevant to your information.

## 6. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, legal obligations and legitimate claim-defence needs.

- **Partner applications:** each application is assigned a retention-review date 24 months after submission. At that review, we assess whether the record should be deleted or anonymised; a longer period is used only where needed for an ongoing application relationship, legal claim, regulatory duty or fraud investigation.
- **Submission, payment and transaction records:** retained for the service relationship and for the accounting, tax, fraud-prevention and claim periods that apply to the record.
- **Contact communications and security records:** retained only for as long as needed to answer the contact, protect the service, investigate an incident or meet a legal obligation.

Where information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it. Some records may need to be retained where law requires it or where they are needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 7. Your rights and choices

Subject to the conditions and limits in UK data-protection law, you may ask for access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction, portability, or information about our use of it. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, including an absolute right to object to direct marketing.

Where a future processing activity relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal will not affect processing already carried out before it was withdrawn.

To exercise a right, email **hello@mintvaultuk.com** with “Privacy rights request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You can also complain to the **Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)** at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). We welcome the opportunity to address a concern first.

## 8. Automated decisions

MintVault does not use Partner-application information to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Partner applications are not approved automatically and do not trigger automatic provisioning or onboarding.

## 9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including access controls, encryption in transit and controlled service-provider access. No system can be guaranteed completely secure; if you believe information has been exposed, contact us promptly.

## 10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our processing changes or the law requires it. The current version and effective date appear at the top of this page. For material changes to a new use of your personal information, we will provide the relevant information before starting that use.
